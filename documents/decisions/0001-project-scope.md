# Decision 0001: Project Scope

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

The project will target `10x+` effective LLM training efficiency as the practical research goal.

The literal `1000:1` framing remains useful as inspiration, but the first rigorous target is `10x` or better because that would already be dramatic progress for commodity-accessible model training.

## Rationale

`10x` is large enough to matter and small enough to test seriously. It allows the project to evaluate stacks of compatible improvements without requiring a single miraculous algorithmic breakthrough.

## Consequences

- Claims must be measured against a conventional dense Transformer baseline.
- Efficiency gains must account for hidden costs.
- The research phase must end in experiment cards, not only a literature review.
- OpenMythos and similar projects are treated as hypothesis aggregates unless their underlying claims are supported by primary sources or experiments.
